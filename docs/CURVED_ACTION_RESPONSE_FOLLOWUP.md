# Curved action-response paths: follow-up candidate

Recorded September 7, 2026 at Steven's request. This preserves an idea for the next clean experimental design; it does not authorize compute or replace the governing experiment plan.

## Existing observation

The [curvature delivery record](../artifacts/geometry_map/action_path_curvature_v1/FINAL_DELIVERY_V1.json) reports bent full-spatial action-response paths and improved internal causal interchange fidelity from curved interpolation relative to endpoint-line reparameterization.

The evidence comes from **four reused Push-T development initial states**, with 32 correlated paths. Directions, radii, imagined horizons, and interpolation targets are not independent replications. Some targets were withheld from an individual curve fit but had already appeared elsewhere in exploration.

The same record also reports that a cubic downstream comparison was worse than the near-chord comparator on three of four states. Actual-history conditioning improved overall encoded-image prediction while increasing bending. Therefore observed curvature does not establish useful steering, a unique semantic manifold, or nonlinear superiority.

## Question to retain

Under matched inputs, endpoints, intervention magnitude, and evaluation context, does modeling action-response curvature improve prediction of withheld responses or selective causal intervention effects over a strong linear/local-linear baseline on fresh trajectories?

This is a candidate question, not a frozen protocol. Physical planning utility is a separate claim.

- Set a meaningful effect size and sample size using paired variability rather than counting paths or candidates as independent.
- Reuse cached features and recorded physical outcomes where valid; identify any missing measurements before launching jobs.
