# What would make an internal planning intervention informative?

Research checked **2026-09-13**, using the arXiv research skill. This is a decision report, not an execution receipt. It was written after the complete B16 analysis; it does not retroactively preregister B or any new outcome. The root-owned successor protocol and execution manifest, once frozen, are authoritative.

## 1. Executive verdict and confidence

**Run a limited pilot:** compare temporally coherent action-donor interventions with equal-delivered-norm random changes inside and outside the literal action encoder's range. Measure reconstruction of a genuine action-input counterfactual as well as disturbance of native goal-cost rankings. Confidence is high that this resolves an identifiable control mismatch; confidence that it will identify a compact physical variable or improve control is low.

The completed [B16 report](ACTION_CONDITION_SPECIFICITY.md) found small permutation-minus-random ranking effects at two separated blocks, B1 and B4, on both tasks and both candidate banks. Excess Spearman loss was approximately 0.007–0.0084; no block met the prespecified 0.05 practical flag. Equal delivered L2 was verified, but equal L2 does not match the action-reachable portion of the conditioning space. That is now the highest-value unresolved confound, not evidence for a unique emergence zone.

## 2. Decision and interpretation of the specification

There are four distinct claims: an edit changes a prediction; it reproduces a particular action counterfactual; it changes an external planner's search; and it improves executed behavior. The repository has evidence for the first and third, a new testable route to the second, and no established fresh net gain for the fourth. These cannot substitute for each other.

In this checkpoint, the [action encoder](../vendor/jepa-wms/app/plan_common/models/AdaLN_vit.py) is an affine `Linear(20, 400)`. Its condition is action-only: `z(a) = W a + b`; proprioception enters the other block stream. Consequently, donor differences satisfy `z(a') − z(a) = W(a' − a)` and lie in `range(W)`, of rank at most 20. For an isotropic random direction in 400 dimensions, the expected fraction of squared norm in this range is `rank(W)/400`, at most 5%. This is a mathematical consequence of the actual architecture, not a discovered manifold or an empirically measured numerical rank.

The complement is `ker(Wᵀ)`, **not** a downstream causal-null space. Perturbations there are unavailable through any action input to this affine encoder but may strongly affect AdaLN. Conversely, membership in the affine range does not guarantee valid action bounds, common training examples, or compatibility with the recipient's visual history.

## 3. Current primary literature and evaluation landscape

Dates below are the latest arXiv versions checked, not publication-priority claims. Reported results are the authors' results; no cross-paper leaderboard is implied.

| Work and checked version | Method, evaluation, and transfer limit |
| --- | --- |
| [JEPA-WM — Terver et al., v4, 2026-09-02](https://arxiv.org/html/2512.24497v4) | Frozen encoders and action-conditioned prediction supply scores to external CEM/gradient planners. Final comparisons use three training seeds and generally 96 episodes per epoch (DROID64, RoboCasa32), with late-epoch aggregation. Code/data/checkpoints are released. Planning outcomes differ from offline embedding MSE. RoboCasa remains outside this project's scope. |
| [COAST — Miao et al., v1, 2026-05-16](https://arxiv.org/html/2605.17144v1) | **Actual activation steering:** a contrastive success/failure conceptor multiplicatively edits a frozen policy/action expert's residual stream. Simulation uses 15 fitting/hyperparameter rollouts and 30 separate evaluation rollouts per task; early MetaWorld/LIBERO checkpoints deliberately provide mixed success/failure examples. The authors also evaluate production RoboCasa checkpoints and real robots. Its control endpoint is useful precedent, but it edits action generation rather than a predictor whose scores feed CEM. Low effective conceptor rank is not the rank of the additive operator `C − I`. |
| [Interpreting Physics in Video World Models — Joseph et al., v1, 2026-02-04](https://arxiv.org/html/2602.07050v1) | IntPhys classification, motion decoding, layerwise probes, and attention ablations concern video **encoders**, not our six-block action-conditioned predictor. Grouped five-fold evaluation supports a depth-dependent physics-decoding pattern. Direction steering uses 240 steering-train videos and 103 separate evaluation-probe videos; the evaluation probe itself is fitted on that latter set. These are decoding/classification interventions, not physical robot-success trials. A similarly shaped depth plot would not establish the same mechanism here. |
| [Manifold Steering — Wurgaft et al., v1, 2026-05-06](https://arxiv.org/html/2605.05115v1) | Fits activation and output-distribution manifolds and evaluates steering paths. Appendix A.6 preserves the off-PCA residual for manifold steering but replaces the full residual for its linear baseline: support changes alongside geometry. MountainCar uses 100 rollouts, position-labelled activation splines, and a deliberately constructed softmax of distances to spline anchors as “behavior,” not task success. These choices motivate matched-support baselines; they do not establish a physical manifold in our predictor. This is the requested exact title, not a similarly named steering paper. |
| [Othello — Nanda, Lee, Wattenberg, v2, 2023-09-07](https://arxiv.org/html/2309.00941v2) | Mine/Yours/Empty, rather than absolute colors, reveals a useful linear board representation. Vector interventions are checked against legal-move consequences on 1,000 edited-board cases; reported average move errors are 0.10 for flips and 0.02 for erasures. This is stronger semantic grounding than agreement with the same model's original scores. It provides a standard for validating a coordinate system, not evidence that robotics uses an analogous one. |
| [Linear Representation Hypothesis — Park, Choe, Veitch, v2, 2024-07-17](https://arxiv.org/html/2311.03658v2) | Counterfactual definitions connect representation, probing and steering; the inner product is part of the formalism, not automatically Euclidean. LLaMA-2-7B experiments use 27 concepts, leave-one-out concept estimates, and 100,000 random word-pair comparisons; supplementary Gemma results illustrate metric dependence. The paper/code support carefully defined controls, not universal linearity of every physical variable. Our Euclidean norm matching is an operational control in the checkpoint's coordinates, not a demonstrated semantic metric. |
| [Interpretability Illusion — Makelov, Lange, Nanda, v2, 2023-12-06](https://arxiv.org/html/2311.17030v2) | Successful learned subspace patches can activate dormant pathways instead of locating an existing feature. Evidence includes a toy model, GPT-2 Small indirect-object identification with unseen templates/entities, and GPT-2 XL factual recall using 40 fact pairs across eight relations. The paper also validates a faithful residual-stream direction: this is not a universal rejection of patching. Its downstream-output kernel is different from our input encoder's orthogonal complement. |
| [Causal Abstraction — Geiger et al., v4, 2025-05-08](https://arxiv.org/abs/2301.04709v4) | A theoretical framework for intervention correspondence and graded faithfulness, not a robotics benchmark. It motivates asking whether an internal intervention reproduces a specified high-level counterfactual. Replacing everything with a known answer establishes a route/check, not a localized explanation. |
| [CAFT — Casademunt et al., v2, 2025-11-09](https://arxiv.org/html/2507.16795v2) | **Concept Ablation Fine-Tuning:** projections operate during fine-tuning and are removed at inference. Insecure-code experiments use 4,500 training examples, 1,000 held-out code queries and five seeds. For Qwen32B, PCA-CAFT reports misalignment 0.51% versus 7.00%, alongside vulnerable-code rate 84.8% versus 91.6%. Test-only ablation is a separate baseline. A frozen JEPA intervention cannot demonstrate this learning-time mechanism or its generalization benefit. |
| [LEAP — Pham and Bera, v1, 2026-09-03](https://arxiv.org/html/2609.03294v1) | Exact title: **Latent Energy Action Planning with World Models**. Frozen LeWM is paired with L-BFGS action optimization, a learned expert-action proposal, and a descriptor decoder trained on 9,000 examples/1,000 held out. Four domains, 100 final trials each after 50-trial tuning: mean success 94.8% versus matched LeWM+CEM 77.5%. Error bars are one binomial standard error, not 95% intervals. The separate paired 50-trial objective ablation is 96.5% full versus 91.0% latent-only. The full-system gain does not isolate energy design; the method is not training-free overall. |
| [Imperfect World Models are Exploitable — Bhamidipaty et al., v2, 2026-05-18](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.15960v2) | Defines exploitation through a reversal between model-implied and true-environment policy preference, and derives limits/safe-horizon results. This supplies a precise failure concept, not evidence that every altered CEM trajectory exploits our checkpoint. Establishing that here requires an environment reference, not simply a lower model cost or a changed proposal. |

The most informative distinction is **intervention target → evaluated consequence**. COAST directly changes a policy; JEPA-WM/LEAP change scoring or optimization around a predictor; Othello supplies exact counterfactual semantics; Physics and Manifold Steering supply representation-level evidence with different readouts. None bridges all these steps for this repository.

## 4. Implementation recommendation and smallest credible path

Use the same 16 development scenarios, both 300-candidate banks, and all six blocks. Reusing these scenarios makes this an explicitly B-informed diagnostic, not independent confirmation. Do not select only B1/B4 after seeing their effects. Do not train a manifold, representation probe, SAE, new policy, or adaptation rule for this control.

The smallest useful successor combines two tests: matched action-range/complement interventions, and faithful reproduction of a **literal H3 action change**. A minimal matrix has native, true-action donor, zero-hook audit, all-block coherent-route audit, and four single-block intervention types across six blocks: donor, ambient random, range random, complement random. That is 28 forwards per bank, or 896 for 16 scenarios and two banks. Additional H3-only versus temporally coherent branches must be separately enumerated before execution; the root-owned registry decides the exact count.

B measured approximately 78 seconds for 28 300-candidate H6 forwards on the staged hardware. The minimal matrix would therefore forecast in roughly 42 single-GPU minutes **if that measured throughput transfers**, excluding model loading, validation and preservation. This is a runtime estimate, not a hardware guarantee. The runtime owner must freeze the actual matrix, time one tagged case without inspecting effects, include idle/setup/storage charges, enforce the user's $2/hour fleet cap, and stop before this diagnostic's <$10 ceiling. No new rentals or GPU authority follow from this report.

## 5. Prioritized hypotheses and alternatives

| Hypothesis | Predicted contrast and falsifying evidence |
| --- | --- |
| H1: B's donor/random gap partly reflects action-range dose rather than donor-specific computation. | Range-random should disturb native ranks more than ambient/complement random at equal delivered L2, reducing the donor advantage. No range-versus-ambient excess with narrow intervals, or an unchanged donor-versus-range gap, weakens this explanation. A mixture of directional alignment and subspace sensitivity remains possible. |
| H2: a localized action-condition route faithfully mediates a genuine action counterfactual. | A coherent single-block donor patch should reproduce the true-action H6 change more accurately than same-dose random controls. Rank disturbance without donor-effect reconstruction weakens the semantic mediation claim. Exact all-block parity alone is an implementation check, not a success for H2. |
| H3: a special depth-localized mechanism explains both specificity and counterfactual fidelity. | A preregistered interior block must exceed the alternatives under corrected paired contrasts, replicate across both banks/tasks, and not simply show large random sensitivity. Two separated peaks, smooth depth leverage, or sensitivity without faithful reconstruction do not establish one emergence zone. Treat this as exploratory unless the protocol explicitly reserves multiplicity and power for localization. |

These hypotheses concern the frozen computation. None predicts that changing model parameters improves future episodes. A later adaptation claim needs execute–observe–update–evaluate-on-later-data comparisons with matched compute and untouched trajectories.

## 6. Experimental protocol, controls and numerical audits

For each recipient candidate, construct a real-input reference by copying only the H3 action block from a deterministic donor and leaving H1/H2/H4–H6 unchanged. Compute its native H6 prediction. With the actual two-step action context, that one logical action appears as the newest condition at H3, `z[:,1]`, and as the older condition at H4, `z[:,0]`. A coherent condition intervention must address both occurrences. Replacing the entire H3 context would also replace H2 and answer a different question.

First confirm that replacing these occurrences across all blocks reproduces the real-input reference, with model parameters and global/private RNG unchanged. This follows the action-only conditioning route and is deliberately an engineering parity control. The scientific arms replace only one named block, B0–B5, at the registered occurrence(s); they do not restore the full block output or transplant donor visual histories.

Compute an SVD of the literal checkpoint matrix `W` in CPU float64. Freeze relative rank tolerance `1e-6`, with `r = count(s > 1e-6 * s_max)`; save all singular values, matrix shape/dtype/hash, rank, tolerance and projector diagnostics. Reject a degenerate rank or empty complement rather than silently changing the protocol. This is numerical range estimation, not fitted behavioral PCA. If singular values straddle the threshold, report that uncertainty; do not tune it against outcomes.

For a private Gaussian vector `g`, use `P g = U_r(U_rᵀg)` and `(I−P)g`. Normalize ambient, range and complement changes separately to the same donor per-candidate L2, preserving a zero donor as exactly zero. Use paired private seeds across these controls, and reuse the registered logical change at both temporal occurrences. Keep field support and number of occurrences matched within a comparison. Never renormalize after observing the endpoint.

Audit delivered, not merely requested, changes after dtype conversion: every candidate's norm, range/complement residual fractions, `||W − P W||/||W||`, projector idempotence and donor residual. Floating-point complements are not exactly null. For range controls, also log the implied minimum-norm raw-action change `W⁺δ` and action-bound violations; do not clip it post hoc, which would change dose. Such violations constrain interpretation even when projection is accurate.

Keep exact input/checkpoint/DINO/source hashes, assigned GPU identity, strict FP32/TF32-off, native zero-hook parity and all completion/archive receipts. Retain all candidate cost arrays and compact H6 difference Gram statistics needed to independently reconstruct endpoint metrics; avoid an unnecessary bulk forecast archive. Never promote a partial cohort or change the candidate registry after effects are viewed.

## 7. Metrics, practical thresholds and decision gates

Primary native-rank endpoint remains tie-aware Spearman under the **official H6 visual MSE + 0.1 × proprioceptive MSE** objective. Report `rho_ambient − rho_range` and `rho_range − rho_donor`; positive values respectively indicate range-specific disruption and remaining donor-specific disruption. Native agreement is not action quality. Native-top-10 overlap, visual-only/proprio-only comparisons, cost margins and actual score changes are secondary.

For counterfactual fidelity, let `d = Y_true_action − Y_native` and `p = Y_patch − Y_native`. Report `F = 1 − ||p − d||² / ||d||²`, separately by output modality and with a declared official-weighted output metric. Zero denotes native-level squared error and one exact reproduction; negative values are meaningful. Preserve the raw squared norms and inner products, plus cosine and normalized residual as secondary metrics. A zero or numerically negligible donor response makes the normalized metric undefined—not zero, and not a reason to drop that scenario selectively. Freeze the numerical floor before execution and retain unnormalized errors for every case.

Suggested **diagnostic**, not efficacy, gates: a task/bank-consistent range-versus-ambient effect of at least 0.002 Spearman units with a simultaneous interval above zero justifies treating ambient noise as an inadequate sole control. A coherent donor reconstruction gain of at least 0.10 over range-random, with a simultaneous interval above zero, warrants a subsequent semantic-validation test. These operational thresholds are proposed prospectively; they are not literature-established physical effect sizes. Failure to clear either gate does not prove equivalence. A claim that the remaining donor gap is negligible requires the entire simultaneous interval inside a prespecified equivalence band, proposed ±0.002, and may be infeasible at n=8.

## 8. Statistical analysis and sample-size plan

The unit is a scenario/trajectory, n=8 per task, not 300 candidates, 400 channels, six blocks or two banks. Compute per-scenario paired contrasts first. Use 20,000 paired scenario-bootstrap draws with one frozen seed; the same draw weights must apply across all layers, banks, controls and endpoints. Keep task strata separate while constructing simultaneous bands over the complete registered primary family. If three contrasts are designated primary across six blocks, two banks and two tasks, that is 72 cells; do not present six-layer-only correction as globally corrected.

Report effect sizes, marginal intervals and simultaneous intervals, all individual scenario values, and undefined counts. The full primary family must be fixed before execution. A localized-peak claim requires direct layer-to-layer contrasts, not merely one block significant versus zero and another nonsignificant. Reserve a separate exploratory family for modalities, top-10 overlap, occurrence ablations and any depth-curve modeling.

This budget does not provide confirmatory power for small effects. As a rough planning calculation under independent approximately normal paired differences, n=8 requires an effect around `(1.96 + 0.84)/sqrt(8) ≈ 0.99` paired standard deviations for 80% power even before multiplicity; small-sample and simultaneous inference demand more. Do not infer power from 300 candidates or claim that bootstrap repetition increases sample size. Use the complete pilot's scenario variance to plan an independently frozen replication with disjoint scenarios and target 80% power at the chosen practical effect; there is no outcome-triggered sample extension in this pilot.

**Presentation is separate from inference.** JEPA-WM Appendix G.2 uses SD across the last ten epoch-level success rates (100 for DROID-trained models) for design-choice plot bars; Table 12 separately describes final-checkpoint variability across three training seeds. Neither is available from our single checkpoint. Local binary-success bars can instead show explicitly labelled one-SE episode-sampling uncertainty, `sqrt(p(1−p)/n)`, while paired-effect intervals remain in the accompanying tables. Smaller displayed bars must not be described as the authors' statistic. For continuous CEM trajectories, use scenario-level `sample_SD/sqrt(n)`, not a Bernoulli formula or head/candidate counts. [Primary definition](https://arxiv.org/html/2512.24497v4#A7.SS2).

## 9. Confounders, limitations and unresolved evidence

Same norm is not same semantic distance; same affine subspace is not the training distribution; same candidate ID after CEM adapts is not the same action; lower goal cost is not physical success. Real donor conditions can also be inconsistent with the recipient's history unless the reference intervention is defined precisely. Single-block transplantation remains an unusual intermediate computation even if its condition vector is action-reachable.

The new controls can establish input-range sensitivity and counterfactual correspondence, but cannot by themselves diagnose a dormant **circuit**. That would additionally require identifying which downstream computation reads the new direction and demonstrating its inactivity under native inputs. Nor does a failed linear donor patch establish a dense nonlinear physical manifold. Existing symmetric center reconstruction is compatible with quadratic behavior and was sensitive to precision; it is not a third-order or intrinsic-dimension measurement.

Search amplification and exploitation are separate. The eight-case CEM addon demonstrates proposal/returned-prefix divergence without executed actions. To establish harmful exploitation, a later fixed trial must compare model preference with actual environment outcomes for the **same** candidate plans. A native-model oracle, a decoder fitted on the same representation, or a score-softmax entropy cannot supply that physical reference.

Finally, these are selected historical development scenarios from one checkpoint. Two candidate banks test candidate-distribution robustness, not new-world generalization. New adaptive weights, fast weights, test-time training, a second model family and fresh physical validation remain separate studies with their own fitting/exposure registries.

## 10. Is this the right research question?

Yes, after replacing “find the planning-emergence zone” with **“which internal action interventions remain faithful when action reachability, temporal addressing and control dose are matched?”** This is a tractable mechanistic question with meaningful negative outcomes. It tests an actual architectural constraint and a genuine model counterfactual before importing a semantic coordinate system or a nonlinear-manifold explanation.

Suggested paper wording: “Our ambient-random control matches activation displacement but not the action encoder's reachable subspace. Because action conditioning is affine, donor differences occupy at most 20 of 400 dimensions. We therefore separate random perturbations inside this range from its orthogonal complement and evaluate whether localized condition patches reproduce a true action-input counterfactual. These checks distinguish steering efficacy from faithfulness; they do not establish physical task improvement or identify a dormant circuit.” This is prospective wording until the successor results are complete.
