## 3. What loop 1 rules out (egg family, this checkpoint)

1. A single predictor site (attn_out/mlp_out/resid_post, layers 0–11, imagined steps 0–2) at route or egg tokens that carries a *patchable* hazard×action state: recovery ≤ 1% everywhere except appearance-only egg-token sites reproduced by main-effect donors.
2. A curved, rotating, or dense-manifold code that a linear LOSO projection would miss (0/324; planted-code positive control passes).
3. A compact conceptor subspace steerable donor-free (max 0.23% recovery, no selectivity).
4. Higher-moment (beyond mean/covariance) distributional structure at step-0 route tokens.
5. A cross-token bilinear relational readout (E4).
6. Trajectory copying / retrieval as the explanation of the small interaction (retrieval gate PASS).
7. A hazard signal in the planner's own currency (energy gap, CEM ranking) on this stimulus.

What is *not* ruled out: a distributed, attention-mediated computation (E1/E2 pending); that the model simply does not build a relational state for a one-patch (35–52 px) egg — the wave-1 planner-currency FAIL and the tiny raw interaction (NMSE 0.96) point this way; that RoboCasa is too far OOD (ratio 1.49, 100% beyond p95) for the DROID-only weights.

## 4. Why loop 2 was started (protocol v0.4)

Failure synthesis: (a) the behaviour-level signal is barely above the retrieval floor and absent in planner currency, so there may be nothing to localize; (b) the egg is one DINO patch — the hazard occupies ~1 token, which makes cross-token relational state unlikely; (c) hazard pixel positions cluster. Loop 2 therefore changes the stimulus first: multi-patch hazards (cup 130–219 px; bowl 320–615 px, 2–3 patches), matched control offsets (`--control-min-m`), same validator and gates, same sealed statistics. Loop-2 status: **cup** 17/29 seeds pass independent label checks, 15 admitted scenes, discovery 5 / confirmation 10 (hash split), GPU stages queued after the Jacobian run; **bowl excluded** — 1/27 seeds pass (44 movement-without-contact, 32 segmentation-projection, 20 visibility failures under the egg-calibrated validator; thresholds were *not* loosened to admit it). A lateral-offset / unseen-action family (C3) was not built.

## Appendix C — Wave 2 localization, egg family, 34 discovery scenes with full H0′ null (2026-09-02 17:30 UTC; `artifacts/cgs_pilot/heldout_v1_merged_w2/mech_w2_localize_interaction_map.json`). Run as a control after the B-gate FAIL; patch/geometry stages skipped.

With n=34 and H0′ cells in every scene, the internal hazard×action interaction at imagined step 0, route (gripper/corridor) tokens, is statistically robust and survives the relational null at many sites: BY rejects 1086-test families broadly; top magnitude-qualified sites are `L00.mlp_out` (t 6.2, FWER p 5e-4, relational t 5.5), `L07.attn_out` (t 6.0, relational t 6.3; the wave-1 candidate, now null-robust), `L03.mlp_out`, `L07.mlp_in`, `L09.attn_out`, `L05.attn_out`. **But** the interaction/action magnitude ratio is 0.05–0.10 at every one of them — the same ~5–10% scale as the behavioural captured-interaction fraction (Appendix B). Reading: the predictor carries a small, consistent hazard×action direction (a real internal signal at n=34) whose magnitude matches its negligible behavioural expression; wave-1 patching of L07.attn_out (0.1% recovery) is consistent with that. A larger internal signal cannot be expected from patching when the model's own output only realises 5% of the interaction — which is the behavioural-target problem in one number.
