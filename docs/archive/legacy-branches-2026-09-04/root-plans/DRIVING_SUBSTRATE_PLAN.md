# Driving JEPA-WM substrate: plan (research agent, 2026-09-02 ~19:40 UTC; user decision pending)

Constraint from `vla_learnings.md`: a released checkpoint can only show whether a model builds the relational state; a "training created it" claim needs matched training arms. This plan builds the arms in from the start (0 / 10 / 50 % collision-future mixture, same data volume, seeds, schedule).

## Recommendation
Train the **JEPA-WMs recipe already used for substrate 1** (frozen DINOv3 ViT-L/16 + depth-12 AdaLN predictor; config `droid_4fpcs_fps4_r256_dv3vitl_asp1_pred_AdaLN_depth12_noprop_repro_2roll_4n.yaml` in `vendor/jepa-wms`) from scratch on self-generated CARLA data with collision futures, on precomputed DINOv3 features. Architecture held fixed → clean domain transfer from substrate 1; same hook stack (`predictor_hooks.py`, `latent_cache.py`, `planner_currency*.py`). A V-JEPA 2-AC-style cell (ViT-L encoder + 24-layer predictor; jepa-wms `vj2ac` config, vjepa2 `app/vjepa_droid`) is the second JEPA cell, after the first passes the B-gate. Fine-tuning the DROID predictor on driving is an ablation only (action embedder must be re-initialised for 2–3-D driving actions). DINO-world has no code/weights; DINO-Foresight is action-free. No open action-conditioned JEPA driving WM with weights exists (re-verified: DriveWorld-VLA 2602.06521 "will release"; Latent-WAM 2603.24581, 2606.12987 unreleased; Drive-JEPA is encoder+planner). Check LeWorldModel (2603.19312) for the "LeJEPA" line.

## Data
Generate our own: CARLA 0.9.15 headless (`-RenderOffScreen`, ~20–25 FPS, CPU-bound) + Leaderboard 2.0 + PDM-Lite expert (`autonomousvision/carla_garage` `data_agent.py`) with ReSim-style ε-perturbed steer / target speed so hazardous and collision episodes are in support (ReSim: 88k 4.9-s clips at 10 Hz, ~half hazardous). Scenarios: Bench2Drive pedestrian scenarios (DynamicObjectCrossing, PedestrianCrossing, VehicleTurningRoutePedestrian) + generic routes. Log ego action (steer, throttle/brake or target speed), min actor distance, contact flag. Do not rely on the Bench2Drive base dataset (400 GB, expert-only, licence contradictory). Determinism: synchronous mode + fixed Δt before world load, world reload per repetition, seeded traffic manager; bit-exact replay verified as in the egg pilot.

## Factorial and gates
≥100 seeds × {H1 pedestrian in lane at TTC ≈ 2 s, H0 sidewalk, H0′ second sidewalk pose at matched pixel displacement} × {A0 brake chunk, A1 constant-throttle chunk}; identical 1-s context; pedestrian ≥3×3 DINOv3 patches; contact only in (H1,A1). Behavioural target: latent interaction NMSE against frozen-encoder latents of the *rendered* true futures (prediction, not decoding), a held-out min-distance probe, and goal-latent CEM brake/go ranking (DINO-WM 100 samples × 10 iterations; JEPA-WMs CEM-L2, context 2; progress-matched goals so goal frames do not carry the hazard). B-gate thresholds as in `behavior_gate.py`. Retrieval control: kNN over *training* clips (CARLA collisions are stereotyped) — the nearest-training-clip audit required by `vla_learnings.md`. ACT-Bench misalignment test: predicted pedestrian latents invariant to ego action.

## Hypotheses
H1 B-gate passes for the 50 % arm; H2 CEM energy DiD > 0 and flip ≥ 0.25 under H1 not H0′; **H3 interaction magnitude scales with collision-future fraction (0/10/50 %) — i.e. the DROID/egg null was an out-of-support effect** (the open question the literature names: ReSim, PhyGenesis 2603.24506, RaWMPC 2602.23259); H4 pedestrian-latent invariance to ego action. Positive control: 50/50 hazard mixture with oversampled (H1,A1). Non-JEPA sanity control: CaRL's Think2Drive reimplementation (DreamerV3 on BEV, weights released).

## Cost
Instance: 4090, ≥16 CPU cores, 64 GB RAM, **300 GB disk** (CARLA ≈ 20 GB + maps 10–15 GB, 20 sim-h of 256² JPEG + BEV/json 30–60 GB, feature cache ≤ 80 GB, checkpoints). Data generation 20–40 wall-h (0.5–1× realtime; 2–3 servers if CPU allows); factorial ~2 h; three predictors 30–60 GPU-h; pipeline ~10 GPU-h. **Total ≈ 100–150 GPU-h, ~2 weeks wall-clock.** Biggest risk: pedestrian too small at 256 px for DINOv3 patches (mitigate: TTC/camera choice, validator patch threshold, 384 px variant). Residual confound: CARLA pedestrians are OOD for DINOv3 vs real video → NeuroNCAP/HUGSIM real-video follow-up still required for the human-relevance claim.
