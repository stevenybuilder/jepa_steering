## Abstract

Mechanistic interpretability of world models is usually motivated by an Othello-style promise: find the internal state that encodes a physically important relation, edit it, and watch the plan change. We argue that the first failure mode of such projects is not a missing mechanism but an *ambiguous behavioural target*: the model may express the relation so weakly that any mechanism search can only return structured nulls, however significant its statistics. We contribute (1) a preregistered *behavioural-target gate* for world-model interpretability in which the *magnitude* of the model's own counterfactual behaviour, not its significance, licenses mechanism search; (2) a frozen *causal geometry sonar* pipeline (counterfactual-validity gate, retrieval control, leave-one-scene-out localization, on-manifold donor patching, and a ladder of geometry classes including conceptors, a cross-token action-Jacobian sonar, and rotating-subspace tracking) that runs unchanged across JEPA-family world models, domains, and architectures; and (3) a *matched-consequence* training design with reversed-assignment arms that share identical images, actions, and future multisets, testing whether a JEPA world model builds a hazard×action relational state when the consequence is in support. ## 1 Introduction

Action-conditioned latent world models such as JEPA-WMs [1], V-JEPA 2-AC [2] and DINO-WM [3] plan by comparing predicted futures under candidate actions. If such a model builds a state that combines "hazard on the route" with "the action I am about to take", then that state is a natural target for interpretability, and editing it is a natural test of causal use. The temptation is to go straight to the mechanism: probe, localize, patch. This paper is about what has to be established first.

**The ambiguous-target problem.** A mechanism search is a test of a hypothesis about *how* the model does something. It is uninterpretable when the model does not clearly do that thing. In our robot-manipulation cell (§3) the predictor carries a hazard×action direction that is statistically robust at family-wise error 5·10⁻⁴ across a thousand-test family, <!-- src: artifacts/cgs_pilot/heldout_v1_merged_w2/mech_w2_localize_interaction_map.json -> earliest_live_gripper_corridor[0].p_maxt_fwer --> yet its magnitude is 5–10 % of the action main effect, exactly the fraction of the true interaction the model's output reproduces. Patching it recovers 0.1 % of the hazard gap. <!-- src: artifacts/cgs_pilot/heldout_v1_merged_w1/mech_w1_patch_step0_results.json (L07.attn_out, gripper_corridor, step 0) --> A significance-licensed search would have called the site "found"; a magnitude-licensed gate says there was nothing to find.

**A lesson from a policy model.** In a preceding project on a vision-language-action policy, a "safety" behaviour was chosen because a released checkpoint could run it, and representation methods were applied before the behaviour had been separated from generic obstacle response with matched physics. Editing succeeded in moving actions and failed specificity; a matched control model had been treated as optional. <!-- src: vla_learnings.md §1 (transcript), §2 (rules adopted) --> Two rules follow and are enforced here: no cell may claim more than its contrast can identify (a released checkpoint supports "this model does / does not build the state", never "this training created it"), and behaviour must be *large and unambiguous* before any geometry is fitted.

**Contributions.** (1) A preregistered behavioural-target gate (B-gate) with magnitude thresholds (§2.2). (2) A frozen causal-geometry-sonar (CGS) pipeline and geometry ladder that transfers across substrates without re-selection (§2.3–2.4). (3) A matched-consequence design with reversed-assignment arms for testing whether an in-support consequence creates the relational state (§2.5). (4) A negative benchmark result on two released JEPA-family world models (§3) and a preregistered driving cell (§4).

## 2 Method

### 2.1 Factorial stimulus and estimand

Every scene is a frozen simulator state with one context frame and a 2×2 factorial of hazard placement × candidate action: H1 (hazard on the action path), H0 (same hazard visible but off-path), A0 (gentle/brake), A1 (aggressive/throttle), plus a null level H0′ (a second off-path pose whose screen displacement from H0 matches the H0→H1 displacement). Contact occurs only in (H1, A1). The simulator supplies the ground-truth future for each cell by bit-exact replay, and the *true interaction vector* is the difference-in-differences of encoder latents, I_true = Δ_h Δ_a z. The model's interaction is the same DiD of its predicted latents; the *relational* DiD subtracts the H0′ contrast. Unit of inference is the scene. <!-- src: experiment_design.md §5.4; scripts/cgs_pilot/protocol.py (paired_did, relational_did) -->

### 2.2 Two gates before any mechanism arm

**Counterfactual-validity gate.** On ≥ 16 held-out scenes: recovered fraction of the true action-induced displacement Rec ≥ 0.5 [4]; interaction cosine > 0 at sign-flip p < 0.05; action-response consistency (ARC) and drift energy reported [5]. Hazard specificity, Rec_h1 − Rec_h0 > 0 and not reversed against H0′, is reported as `verdict_with_specificity`. <!-- src: scripts/cgs_pilot/counterfactual_validity_gate.py; artifacts/cgs_pilot/heldout_v1_merged_w1/cf_gate_sonar_all.json -> criteria -->

**Behavioural-target gate (B-gate).** Preregistered 2026-09-02 16:40 UTC, before any model output of the next family, and applied retroactively to the first family (disclosed in Appendix B). Tier 1 (all required): n ≥ 16; median interaction NMSE ≤ 0.75, i.e. the model reproduces ≥ 25 % of the true interaction energy; Rec_h1 − Rec_h0 > 0 at sign-flip p < 0.05 and not reversed by H0′. Tier 2 (required for a planner-level claim): CEM ranking flip rate H1 vs H0 ≥ 0.25, or a normalised energy gap > 0 at p < 0.05. FAIL means the mechanism arms are not run and the finding is "no unambiguous behavioural target on this stimulus/model"; the remedy is a stimulus or model change, never another interpretability method. <!-- src: experiment_design.md "Behavioural-target gate (B-gate)"; scripts/cgs_pilot/behavior_gate.py thresholds --> The distinction from the validity gate is deliberate: the validity gate asks whether counterfactuals are trustworthy; the B-gate asks whether there is enough behaviour to explain.

### 2.3 The frozen sonar pipeline

Stages run in fixed order on discovery scenes; confirmation scenes are sealed until a site band is frozen. (i) *Retrieval control*: the model's interaction cosine must beat trajectory-copying baselines (other-scene copy-delta, nearest-training-clip kNN, scene-blind action→delta ridge, persistence) with scene-clustered CIs. (ii) *Localization*: at every predictor site × imagined step × token group, a leave-one-scene-out projection of the held-out scene's interaction onto the direction fitted on the other scenes, with sign-flip max-T over sites and Benjamini–Yekutieli for the descriptive map; a site is a *candidate* only if its interaction/action magnitude ratio ≥ 0.05 and it survives the H0′ relational null. (iii) *On-manifold donor patching*: real counterfactual donors (H1↔H0 activations at the site and token group) with sham, norm-matched random, time-shifted, unrelated-site, main-effect-only, wrong-token-group and H0′-donor controls; recovery is measured as the fraction of the true hazard gap moved in prediction space, and selection requires ≥ 30 % recovery with controls < 10 %. Mahalanobis distance to the receiver distribution is reported as an off-manifold diagnostic (our choice; [6] supplies only the harmless-vs-pernicious divergence distinction). Bands rather than single indices are claimed, our derivation from the single-input mediation variance shown in [7]. <!-- src: experiment_design.md "arXiv research audit" bullets (Statistics, Patching); scripts/cgs_pilot/localize_interaction.py, patch_site.py -->

**A structural fact that shapes the search.** In the JEPA-WM predictor the action enters in exactly one way: `adaLN_modulation(action_encoder(a))` yields (shift, scale, gate) per block, broadcast identically to all tokens; there are no action tokens. Hence any change of hazard-token content produces a nonzero "hazard×action interaction" at every nonlinear site by elementwise algebra, and a *relational* state can only be created across tokens, through attention after an action-modulated block. This is why analysis is per token group (hazard / route / background), why the H0′ null is mandatory, and why the geometry ladder includes a cross-token derivative that is artifact-free by construction. <!-- src: "JEPA rep geometry.md" §1; claude_handoff/HANDOFF.md "arXiv research audit" (Architecture facts) -->

### 2.4 Geometry ladder

Because a null on a fixed direction is not a null on all geometry, the pipeline tests an ordered ladder of geometry classes, each with its own null: fixed direction (LOSO projection); low-rank subspace (rank sweep for AUROC); model-native contrast subspace "sonar" (LOSO k-dimensional subspace of true interaction vectors, with random-subspace, action-main-effect-subspace and quartet-shuffled controls); coordinate-frame tournament (world / camera / image / gripper / path-relative); curved or rotating codes (covariate-local vs global projection, RSA against hazard bearing, kNN / kernel / Jacobian-local readouts, planted-rotating-code positive control); bilinear relational readout ŷ = z_routeᵀ W z_hazard (rank 1–2, LOSO, quartet-shuffled null); conceptors in the COAST sense [8] fitted on residualised DiD vectors with C_safety = C_int ∧ ¬C_action ∧ ¬C_null, matched-spectrum-random and rank-one controls, and a donor-free multiplicative intervention; higher-order distributional sonar (Cramér–Wold sliced-W2 on frozen projections before and after moment matching); rotating-subspace tracking (principal angles, Procrustes transport across layers and steps); and a **cross-token action-Jacobian sonar** J_g = ∂P_g/∂a on route tokens with ΔJ_rel = [J(z_H1) − J(z_H0)] − [J(z_H0′) − J(z_H0)], summarised by S_F = ‖ΔJ_rel‖_F / ‖J(z_H0)‖_F against random-token and sign-randomised perturbation controls and confirmed by finite differences. An energy identity ties the sonar to the planner: dE_hz/da·(a₁−a₀) = 2(P_H1 − P_H0)ᵀ[J(z_H1) − J(z_H0)](a₁−a₀). Conceptual quotienting of the action-shared component follows [9]; the sign-flip and max-T machinery is Westfall–Young [10, 11, 12]. <!-- src: "JEPA rep geometry.md" §2.1–2.7; scripts/cgs_pilot/geometry_*.py, action_jacobian_sonar.py, sonar_metrics.py -->

### 2.5 Matched-consequence design (reversed-assignment arms)

A released checkpoint cannot attribute a state to a training signal. The driving cell therefore trains two small predictors on frozen DINOv3 features that differ in exactly one thing, and the arms are built so their training *futures* are the same multiset. **Arm A:** pedestrian is a physical collider, an envelope-matched inanimate object (same collision cylinder, position, size) is intangible. **Arm B:** the reverse. Every training clip exists in both arms with identical images up to first contact, identical actions, and the identical multiset of colliding / non-colliding futures; only which identity the consequence attaches to differs. The registered estimand is the arm × identity × action three-way interaction on (i) the predicted-latent interaction and (ii) planner ranking (a double dissociation is predicted), and the geometry prediction is that the hazard-*appearance* subspace is shared across arms while the hazard×action *relational* subspace attaches to pedestrian tokens in A and object tokens in B, with matching geometry after Procrustes transport. This replaces an earlier SOLID-vs-GHOST design whose future multisets were unequal, and an earlier 0/10/50 % support-dose design. <!-- src: experiment_design.md "Protocol v0.7" and "v0.7 amendment" -->

### 2.6 Statistics and preregistration discipline

Scene is the unit; sign-flip permutation for interaction and patch effects; max-T across sites for family-wise error; BY for descriptive maps; scene-clustered bootstrap for CIs only. Discovery/confirmation split by a fixed seed hash; confirmation seeds sealed and, in substrates 1–2, never opened because no band was ever frozen. Calibration seeds are excluded from inference. Every threshold is fixed in the gate scripts; a significant p with zero recovery is reported as an artifact, following the proxy-vs-objective warning of [13]. <!-- src: LOOP1_SUMMARY.md §1; "JEPA rep geometry.md" §3 -->

## 5 Limitations

*Out-of-distribution substrates.* Substrates 1–2 probe DROID-only checkpoints on RoboCasa renders beyond the DROID p95; the negative result is "these models do not build the state on this stimulus", not "JEPA cannot". <!-- src: LOOP1_SUMMARY.md §3 --> *Small n and few action chunks.* 12–34 discovery scenes; only two distinct action chunks and no unseen-action family; hazard pixel positions cluster across scenes (72/112 cells within 2 px of another scene's centroid), controlled within scene by DiD and H0′ but not across scenes. <!-- src: heldout_v1_merged_w1/contamination.json (action_audit, duplicate_audit) --> *Retroactive B-gate.* The gate was preregistered after the first family's results were seen (before any output of later families); loop-1 arms are therefore controls, not tests. *Simulator realism.* MetaDrive pedestrians and cones are rigid cylinders; the consequence is dominated by ego deceleration because the hazard body is immovable (a knock-over variant is future work). <!-- src: experiment_design.md v0.7 tolerances --> *Small predictor.* Width 512 is below the 1024-d DINOv3 features and bottlenecks the predictor embedding; depth 6 vs the released depth 12. <!-- src: claude_handoff/HANDOFF.md "~22:30 UTC trainer ready" --> *Diagnostic defects.* The nHSIC/CKA permutation null was degenerate (p = 1.0 everywhere) and is reported as uninformative. <!-- src: LOOP1_SUMMARY.md §2 higher-order row --> *Driving cell: what the positive result is not.* (a) **Template capture.** The stimulus is stereotyped by design (hazard always 9.0 m ahead; every stimulus covariate degenerate in range), the DiD field is nearly one direction, and after template removal the models' residual cosine is ≈ 0 while another scene's residual scores 0.4–0.6: "captured 50–69 %" certifies that the predictor learned the shared consequence template, not scene-specific generalisation of it; a varied-distance/speed factorial is the fix and is listed as the next experiment. ## 6 Conclusion

Interpretability of world models needs a licence that significance cannot give. A preregistered magnitude gate turned what would have been a "localized hazard×action site" into the correct statement that two released JEPA-family world models express 0–6 % of a hazard×action interaction on a manipulation factorial, and that every geometry class in a broad ladder is consistently null on such a stimulus. The same frozen pipeline, plus a matched-consequence training design that keeps images, actions and future multisets identical across arms, now has a driving cell where the consequence is in support by construction and where the gate passes: 6/6 trained models clear the B-gate under their own physics and 12/12 cross-truth cells fail it, with the caveat that a ghost hazard in lane also clears the within-arm gate through its visual pass-through interaction. <!-- src: artifacts/cgs_pilot/drive_results_summary.json -> seed_replication --> On the seed-0 pair the frozen sonar transfers unchanged and reads both a localized and a distributed instance of the same relational computation: in arm A a single step-0 site whose donor patch recovers 72 % of the hazard gap (identity-specific, not token-group selective; L3 on discovery, confirmation pending), in arm B no sufficient site but identity-specific relational Jacobian structure at every layer (L2), and in both a hazard-induced change of the action Jacobian 2–4× a random perturbation. <!-- src: drive_mech/armA_seed0/patch_step0/patch_results.json; drive_mech/armB_seed0/patch_step0/patch_results.json; drive_mech/*/jacobian_sonar/jacobian_sonar.json --> At the subspace level the hazard×action geometry is one shared direction per identity (hard rank 1 in both arms; transported appearance cosine 0.68 vs 0.08 random) and the arms differ in what they write along it: the registered double dissociation of relational energy is null and identity-swapped transport follows appearance as often as consequence, so coordinates are shared while content differs. <!-- src: drive_geometry/seed0/cross_arm_map.json -> registered_tests i–iv --> The same pipeline shows that what the gate certified on this stereotyped stimulus is the consequence's template rather than its scene-specific form (model residual cosine ≈ 0.1 or below vs 0.4–0.6 for another scene's residual). <!-- src: drive_relational/relational_transport.json --> The reading we take from the two substrates is that support, not architecture, determines whether the relational consequence exists: predictors of the same JEPA family capture 0–6 % of it when the consequence is absent from training and 50–69 % when it is present. What remains open is whether steering the shared direction changes the planner's choice on sealed scenes, and whether a varied-distance factorial yields a scene-specific rather than a template relational state.
