## 1. Where we looped, with evidence

Three days (Phase-0 on 2026-09-01; paper App. B), seven protocol versions, about eighteen instruments.

- **Egg (v0.3–v0.4, 09-01/02).** JEPA-WM DROID captured 4.5–6.5 % of the hazard×action interaction at n = 21/34/66; V-JEPA 2-AC 0 %. Every mechanism arm null: best patch 0.1 %, conceptor intervention 0.23 %, bilinear AUROC exactly 0.500, Jacobian S_F 0.16 below the random control 0.32 (`LOOP1_SUMMARY.md` §2, App. D–E). Cup/bowl added (v0.4), retired (v0.5).
- **Driving pivot (v0.6 → v0.7, 09-02 evening).** A support-dose design was replaced within hours by reversed-assignment arms; six width-512/depth-6 predictors on cached DINOv3 features; 120 scenes, 54 discovery.
- **v0.7 (09-03).** B-gate PASS 6/6 (captured 0.50–0.69), but the ghost identity passes too (0.37–0.59); cross-truth 12/12 FAIL; `L03.mlp_out` patch 72 % with wrong-group 0.61, time-shift 0.47, main-effect-only 0.33; arm B no site; cross-arm tests (i)–(iv) null, conceptors hard rank 1; residual cosine ≈ 0 vs copy-delta 0.4–0.6; Jacobian on 8 scenes, object-insertion cosine 0.64–0.75.
- **v0.8 (same day).** 8/10 m, 31 discovery scenes; within-distance residual cosine again ≈ 0. **v0.9** generator and the identity-contrast geometry script ("METHOD fix" for the cross-arm null) both written that evening.
- **Still absent:** any closed-loop rollout ("no rollout has ever hit or avoided a hazard", F10) and the steered-planner table (`artifacts/cgs_pilot/drive_coast_table` does not exist; the box job waits on `CROSS_ARM_DONE`).

Instrument succession, each added after a null: DiD subspaces, LOSO sonar, localization, patch sweep, focus battery, curved-manifold check, geometry tournament, conceptor arm, higher-order W2, bilinear probe, Jacobian sonar, modulation patching, cross-truth, relational transport / residual cosine, cross-arm Procrustes tests, conceptor cross-similarity, identity-contrast geometry, residual-by-distance, Panel B.

The pattern:

1. **Hypothesis-first conjunction target.** The registered estimand is arm × identity × route × action (paper §2.5) — the four-part conjunction that sank the VLA project (`vla project overnight failures.md` §1). A null cannot say which conjunct failed.
2. **Open-loop proxies.** Captured fraction, CEM flip rate, progress-goal ranking: predicted latents, never an outcome. The flip rate is identity-blind by construction (pedestrian-vs-cone flip 0.0, F3).
3. **Instrument after every null; statistics outrunning signal.** Every step-0 site sits at the max-T floor p = 1/2001, so localization is non-discriminative (F13). Significance licensed search — the failure the paper's own abstract warns against.
4. **Stereotyped stimulus → rank-1 template.** Hazard always 9.0 m, ego speed 8.27–8.31 m/s, 432/432 cells within 2 px of another scene's centroid; PC1 0.54–0.67. A template cannot resolve identity, so tests (ii)–(iii) were null before they ran.
5. **n = 54** scenes (53 for mechanism, 8 for Jacobian, 31 for v0.8), three seed pairs (p floor 0.125); ten concurrent chains on two boxes with 9 GB disk produced an alias defect, a seed leak, a sign inversion, a false marker and a watchdog kill.

## 2. What COAST actually did (arXiv:2605.17144; Miao, Kim, Yang, Ungar, May 2026)

| | COAST | Us |
|---|---|---|
| Label | episode success/failure, "defined either by simulation or by real world outcomes" | DiD of predicted latents; no episode label |
| Object | one conceptor C = R(R+α⁻²I)⁻¹ at one action-expert layer, action tokens mean-pooled; C_success ∧ ¬C_failure, "roughly one percent of the total hidden dimension", "strictly multi-dimensional, rather than rank-one" | 18 sites × 3 token groups × 3 steps; subspaces, conceptors, Procrustes maps, Jacobians |
| Intervention | h′ = h[(1−β)I + βC_steer]ᵀ, global or per-step | donor patches; conceptor steering on the egg only |
| Endpoint | held-out task success rate | captured fraction, flip rate, ranking |
| Samples | 15 fitting + 30 held-out rollouts per task; 45 + 10 + 7 + 3 tasks | 54 scenes, one factorial |
| Checkpoints | "early fine-tuning checkpoints that produce the mix of successes and failures needed for contrastive fitting" | six fully trained predictors; whether they ever fail is unknown |
| Hyperparameters | layer by quota, aperture by overlap, β by sweep ("93 % of oracle" at 3–8 % of the grid) | preregistered thresholds on many derived quantities |
| Headline | π0.5 LIBERO-10 0.43 → 0.80; π0-FAST 0.62 → 0.84; MetaWorld 0.69 → 0.94; real robot +40 % | none yet |
| Not claimed | no circuit or feature localization; "tasks with nearly disjoint success and failure subspaces show smaller gains" | localized-vs-distributed mechanism from one seed |

COAST's elegance is not the conceptor: an unambiguous label, one operator at one site, the endpoint people care about, repeated across 65 tasks. Its gap is ours to fill: an outcome-only label "can capture task phase, scene identity, motor program, or generic competence" (local `coast.md`); the paper never separates safety from generic competence.

## 3. Saining Xie's methodology, grounded

- **One controlled roadmap from a strong simple baseline.** ConvNeXt: "We gradually 'modernize' a standard ResNet toward the design of a vision Transformer, and discover several key components that contribute to the performance difference along the way" (arXiv:2201.03545). One change at a time; a trajectory, not a tournament.
- **Scale as evidence, one standard metric.** DiT: "DiTs with higher Gflops … consistently have lower FID" (arXiv:2212.09748). A trend, not a p-value.
- **Diagnose the bottleneck, simplest fix, standard endpoint.** REPA: "one main bottleneck … lies in effectively learning these representations … a straightforward regularization" → 17.5× faster SiT training, FID 1.42 (arXiv:2410.06940). RAE replaces the VAE with frozen "pretrained representation encoders (e.g., DINO, SigLIP, MAE)" (arXiv:2510.11690) — our cached DINOv3 features are already this move.
- **Control the data, then scale.** Web-SSL trains SSL on the same MetaCLIP data as CLIP and finds it "does not saturate even after scaling up to 7B" (arXiv:2504.01017).
- **Build the benchmark the shortcut cannot solve.** Cambrian-1: "design choices for vision components are often insufficiently explored"; CV-Bench exists because benchmarks under-weight visual grounding (arXiv:2406.16860). Voxel51's report of his CVPR 2025 remarks: "We need benchmarks that force models to develop actual visual intelligence, not just leverage language priors."
- **World models, his version.** Xie is *not* a V-JEPA 2 author (arXiv:2506.09985 author list checked). His world-model work is Cambrian-S: "scale alone is insufficient … a self-supervised next-latent-frame predictor leverages surprise (prediction error) to drive memory and event segmentation," a proof-of-concept on one new benchmark (arXiv:2511.04670).
- **Experiments over theory-in-the-head.** On the "is DiT mathematically wrong" critique: this "requires making assumptions, conducting experiments, and verifying them, rather than doing science in a pretend way in your head" (X post, reported by 36kr).
- **Taste.** Seven-hour interview (Zhang Xiaojun podcast; Lin Cong's notes, April 2026, paraphrased): a paper should help us understand something, which matters more than numbers; bad taste is chasing external markers. CVPR 2025 talk "Research as an Infinite Game" (his X post with slides); secondary summaries say DiT/SiT were first rejected for "lack of novelty" — slides not verified. No primary source exists for a "make it work first" quote; not attributed.

Through-line: simplest strong baseline, change one thing, measure on the real endpoint, scale until the trend is visible, let the benchmark carry the argument.

## 4. The Xie-style redesign of this experiment

**Same label and endpoint as COAST.** Closed-loop outcome under the model's own CEM planner in MetaDrive: safe-pass = goal reached, no contact, progress ≥ 0.9 of the hazard-free rollout; else collision or progress-failure (`behavioral_design_v08.md` §4, never built). Discovery labels come from unsteered rollouts on v0.9 scenes.

**One table, COAST in row 1, our operators as the contribution** (the Step 8 ladder in `cross model design jepa.md`, collapsed):

1. unsteered; 2. identity hook (bit-identical);
3. **faithful COAST** — C_safe ∧ ¬C_collision at one layer chosen by quota, tokens mean-pooled, β by sweep;
4. **Sonar relational conceptor** — the identity-attached component factorised out of the outcome geometry by the matched arms: express arm B's outcome conceptor in arm A coordinates through the hazard-free Procrustes map T; C_rel^A = C_out^A ∧ ¬(T C_out^B Tᵀ), C_gen = C_out^A ∧ (T C_out^B Tᵀ). C_gen is what both arms share (in-lane presence, braking); C_rel is what only the consequence-carrying identity wrote;
5. **cross-arm transported operator** — C_rel^A carried into arm B by T; it helps the pedestrian there only if it carries identity rather than "brake when something is in lane";
6. **AdaLN modulation operator** — conditional low-rank edit on the (shift, scale, gate) path, the only place the action enters;
7. **minimum-distortion steering** with preservation constraints: maximise relational energy subject to ‖δ‖_N ≤ ε, D_action ≤ τ, Mahalanobis ≤ τ_d; hazard-free, H0/H0′ and ghost-identity behaviour within TOST margins (5 pp success, 0.10 on rates);
8–14. matched-spectrum random, rank-one CAA, reversed, wrong-layer, wrong-group, wrong-step, sham.

**The decisive comparison is selectivity, not success rate.** gain(cell) = steered − unsteered safe-pass; S = gain(solid in lane) − gain(ghost in lane); hazard-free and off-lane cells are equivalence constraints. Predictions: COAST raises safe-pass in both in-lane cells (generic avoidance — the VLA project's "human and matched-object outcomes identical", now expected rather than feared); C_rel raises it on the solid identity with S ≥ +10 pp and CI excluding 0; C_gen reproduces COAST; transported C_rel^A raises pedestrian safe-pass in arm B; the modulation operator meets the same rule at lower distortion. Bidirectional: suppressing C_rel lowers safe-pass on the solid identity only. Monotone dose over {0.25, 0.5, 1.0}.

**The figure.** One panel: safe-pass in the 2 × 2 (arm × in-lane identity), unsteered vs COAST vs C_rel, with CIs; beside it S versus β for both. Everything else is appendix.

**Data scale.** MetaDrive is free; the v0.9 generator already randomises distance, prefix throttle, lateral offset, FOV and settle. Target 500 scenes × 2 identities × 2 arms × 2 CEM seeds ≈ 4,000 episodes (COAST: ≈ 2,000 held-out). Fit on ≥ 30 successes and ≥ 30 failures per arm; test on ≥ 200 episodes per cell. If unsteered solid-in-lane safe-pass is outside 20–80 %, raise training clips from 1,598/arm to ~10k before any operator is fitted.

**Model scale.** Width 512 is not too small for a conceptor (d = 512 against thousands of pooled states). It may be too small to drive; measure it: 512/6, 768/8, 1024/12, one seed per arm, safe-pass against Gflops, pick the smallest usable model. Do not argue.

**Keep as second-stage tools:** cross-truth, the hazard-free Procrustes transport (row 5 needs it), residual cosine as a stimulus-variety check, single-layer donor patching, identity-contrast geometry inside the outcome subspace, Panel B through the same table. **Drop from the main line:** 18-site × 3-group × 3-step localization, cross-arm tests (i)–(iv), Jacobian sonar, bilinear, higher-order, curved-manifold, conceptor cross-similarity, relational transport, Panel C, planted-mechanism control, egg anchors in every table.

**Execution order (RTX 3090, $0.17/h).**

1. Closed-loop CEM adapter + unsteered gate, six models, v0.9 scenes (~1,200 episodes). ≈ 3 GPU-h. Stop: no model in 20–80 % → step 2 before anything geometric.
2. Scale sweep (3 sizes × 2 arms; ~10k clips if needed). ≈ 8 GPU-h. Stop: choose the smallest usable model; report the trend either way.
3. Faithful COAST row, discovery fit, held-out test with random/CAA/sham. ≈ 3 GPU-h. Stop: if COAST gives < +10 pp on every in-lane cell, publish "outcome conceptors do not transfer to a JEPA planner at this scale" and stop.
4. Rows 4–7 plus controls, one opening of sealed scenes, zero refitting; the selectivity figure. ≈ 5 GPU-h. Stop: if S for C_rel is indistinguishable from COAST's S, the claim is "editable outcome geometry, no separable identity component" — reported, not rescued.
5. Only if 4 passes: dose/bidirectionality, single-layer patch, Panel B replication. ≈ 6 GPU-h.

≈ 25 GPU-h, about $5.

**Headline that would survive:** "In JEPA world models trained with the collision consequence in support, an outcome conceptor (COAST) raises closed-loop safe-pass on any in-lane hazard, whereas the relational component isolated by reversed-assignment arms raises it only on the identity that carried the consequence in training, transfers across arms through a shared coordinate map, and leaves hazard-free driving unchanged."

## 5. Stop doing / genuinely novel

Stop:
- adding an instrument after a null — the next null gets a stimulus or a scale change;
- running geometry before a closed-loop outcome exists;
- three-way-interaction estimands as the first target;
- 54-scene, 8-scene, single-seed mechanism claims;
- ten concurrent chains on 9 GB of disk — one chain, one marker, one box;
- writing the paper's localized-vs-distributed section from seed 0;
- protocol versions faster than results (v0.6 → v0.9 in ~30 hours).

Genuinely novel, absent from COAST:
- **Reversed-assignment arms**: identical pixels, actions and futures, consequence attached to different identities — a training-origin claim no released checkpoint can support.
- **Factorisation of outcome geometry** into a generic in-lane/brake component and an identity-attached relational component, with the ghost identity as the built-in selectivity control COAST never has.
- **Cross-architecture recurrence** on identical features and clips (AdaLN vs action-token), where COAST compares policies with different training.

None of these needs the current instrument stack. They need the label.
