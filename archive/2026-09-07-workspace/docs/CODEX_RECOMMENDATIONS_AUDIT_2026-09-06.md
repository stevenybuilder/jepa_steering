# Audit of Codex recommendations — 2026-09-06

Scope: every forward-looking recommendation left by the overnight Codex sessions (September 5 requirements audit, the `decision_evidence` matrix, `autoresearch_steering_20260906/FINDINGS.md`, the arXiv decision note, the Reach pilot note, and the deferred-work register), checked against the power-annotated [geometry map v11](../artifacts/geometry_map/reach_wall_v1/map-v11/GEOMETRY_MAP.md). Verdicts are **adopt**, **modify**, **defer**, or **drop**. No new experiment was run for this audit.

## The one fact that changes every verdict

Every intervention test run overnight used **4 reused initial states** (Push-T) or **5–8 starts** (Reach). The exact two-sided sign-test floor at n=4 is p=0.125, and the 80%-power minimum detectable effect (MDE) at n=4 is 1.9–14 pp against a 2 pp gate. The measured cost of one start is seconds of GPU (the whole 27-configuration Push-T campaign used 22 GPU-seconds of model time; the 8-start Reach pilot used 76 s). The campaign was therefore limited by design, not by compute: the harnesses exist, the split rules exist, and n could have been 50–100× larger at negligible cost.

Two tests were adequately powered and are genuine negatives: the Reach output-correction pilots (n=8, MDE 1.6 mm vs a 5 mm gate, observed −0.6 to −0.7 mm vs native) and the pooled CEM elite-mean-vs-best comparison (n=8, MDE 1.2 pp vs 2 pp gate, observed +0.1 pp). Everything else is either zero-variance (the edit never changed the argmin) or underpowered.

## What survives as a plan

1. **Power the existing causal harness** (rec. 4). Reach predictor block-3 subspace forks at ≥48 development starts spanning approach, contact, and post-contact, with the sham and identity arms already implemented. Endpoint: paired 15-step progress (mm). Gate frozen at 5 mm, or narrow the claim to the 2 mm level the design can resolve. Expected cost: minutes.
2. **Objective-alignment map** (recs. 7, 9, 10). For the same states, physically execute every candidate in a fixed bank and record native cost, oracle-future cost, and native success. Then ask the mechanistic question: which encoder directions dominate the latent goal distance, and are they the task-relevant ones the representation map found (progress, wall distance) or nuisance (background, agent pose)? This is the only line where the model has already done something unexpected (oracle futures still misrank) and there is a concrete "why" to look for inside the model.
3. **Stop the operator search** (recs. 6, 8). Twenty-nine configurations, all null; the one powered pilot is a clean negative. Write it up as the negative result with the power ledger; do not add estimators.
