## What completion will mean

Reach and Reach-Wall, 96 canonical development scenarios each, all five existing
conditions. One common first-CEM population of 300 action sequences per state,
H6 prediction, chosen 15-elementary-step physical prefix. All candidate scores
and actual actions are retained. This is a short decision diagnostic, not a new
960-full-episode success panel or an author-equivalent full CEM evaluation.

The primary endpoint is paired end-effector distance to the expert goal after
the prefix, with eight simultaneous comparisons. Outcome reuse is permitted
only for identical chosen candidates within a scenario and is explicitly logged.

## Non-steering directions supported by the existing data

| Direction | Existing evidence | Important limitation |
|---|---|---|
| Forecast metrics versus decision quality | Corrected offline metrics and the complete controlled MetaWorld rollout panel | Old logs contain action hashes, not recoverable candidate rankings; this diagnostic fills that specific gap |
| Reliability of world-model evaluation | Lineage audit, corrected splits, matched controls, both precisions | An evaluation/reproducibility contribution, not a new robot-control method; never count repeated prefixes as independent demonstrations |
| Forecast stability across horizons and tasks | Existing H1–H6 prediction errors and BF16/FP32 comparisons | Must analyze the full registered scope, not select favorable tasks or endpoints; any new analysis is exploratory |
| Geometry of responses to nearby action inputs | Existing four-donor/omitted-center reconstruction diagnostics | Accurate internal reconstruction is not better physical prediction or control; the symmetric cubic estimate does not uniquely demonstrate third-order dynamics |

The strongest proposed direction is decision-quality auditing. Existing edits
can serve as controlled perturbations, with the contribution centered on what
the model's scores do or do not tell a planner, rather than on a steering recipe.
JEPA-WM Appendix G.3 and WorldModelGym already discuss this broad problem, so
the generic gap is **not** a novelty claim. A useful paper still needs a specific
measured finding and a clearly bounded claim.
